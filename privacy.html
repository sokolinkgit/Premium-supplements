Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated 20 October 2026

This notice explains how Premium Supplements (“we”, “us”) handles the information you share with us when you browse this website or place an order. We keep it short because we keep it simple: we only ask for what we need to answer your questions and deliver your parcel.

What we collect

  • Order details — your name, phone number, county, the product you are interested in and any message you send us.
  • Delivery details — the town, pickup point or address you give us so a courier can reach you.
  • Conversation records — the WhatsApp or phone conversation we have with you, so we can follow up and resolve issues.

This website does not use cookies, advertising pixels or third-party analytics, and the product search and review filters run entirely in your browser.

How the forms work

The order buttons and the contact form on this site do not send anything to a server. They simply open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message that you can read, edit and choose to send. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in WhatsApp.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and confirm pricing, stock and suitability.
  • To pack and dispatch your order and share tracking with you.
  • To follow up after delivery and help with any questions.
  • To keep records required of us by law.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We only share what a courier needs to deliver your parcel, and we remove your details from our active chat records when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Message us on WhatsApp or call 0113 111 330 and we will action it.

Health information

Please do not send medical records or sensitive health details over WhatsApp. We can answer product questions and point you to a qualified health professional, but we do not provide diagnosis or treatment.
